What changed — Enhanced version
Section headers
Pill badges replace plain text + dot. Each category gets its own color background so sections feel distinct at a glance.
Card left border
4px colored left border on each card anchors it to its section. Scannable at speed — no need to read the header.
Icon background tint
Icon containers use a light tint of the section color instead of flat gray — adds depth without being loud.
+ button color
The + button picks up the section color instead of always red. Reinforces category without changing the tap target size.
