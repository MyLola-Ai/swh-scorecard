## App Privacy (Apple's questionnaire)

When App Store Connect asks "Do you or your third party partners collect data from this app?" answer **Yes**.

### Data Linked to the User

- **Contact Info → Email Address** — Used for: App Functionality (account), Product Personalization (welcome emails)
- **Contact Info → Phone Number** — Used for: App Functionality (SMS reminders, optional, opt in only)
- **Contact Info → Name** — Used for: App Functionality (display in app)
- **User Content → Other User Content** — Used for: App Functionality (your activity logs, contacts, notes)
- **Identifiers → User ID** — Used for: App Functionality (account)

### Data Not Collected

Confirm none for: Health & Fitness, Financial Info, Location, Sensitive Info, Contacts (your phone contacts), Browsing History, Search History, Purchases, Audio Data, Photos or Videos (the card scanner sends one image to Anthropic for OCR but does not store it).

### Data Used to Track You

**None.** SWH Scorecard does not track users across apps or websites owned by other companies.
