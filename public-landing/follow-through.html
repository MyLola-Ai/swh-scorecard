Why follow-through wins

The handshake is the seed. Follow through is the harvest.

Selling at first contact kills the relationship before it starts. The handshake's only job is to earn the next conversation. What happens over the next six weeks is what turns a name in your phone into a referral partner for life.

The problem was never meeting people. It's what happens after. Memory fails. Good intentions slide. The person you were genuinely excited to meet becomes a business card in a drawer. The 8-Step System exists so that never happens again, because systems beat willpower every week of the year.

"Trust is not built in grand speeches. Trust is built in small kept commitments."

The System

Eight steps. Forty-five days. Every contact.

Each step is timed, purposeful, and light enough to actually do. The CRM queues the next one so your only job is to show up.

1
Day 1–2 · while the handshake is warm
Great Meeting You
A short, genuine note that sounds like you, not a template. No pitch, no agenda. You met, it was good, you wanted to say a proper hello. This one message separates you from the 95% who never send anything.
2
Day 5 · give them a reason to stay close
Invite to The Networking Wire
Bring them into the community. A shared orbit means you don't have to manufacture reasons to stay in touch, the relationship has a place to live.
3
Day 7 · be where they already are
Connect on Social
LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram. A follow and a genuine comment keeps you in their feed and their memory between real conversations.
4
Day 10–14 · make the ask easy to say yes to
Invite to a 1-to-1
Coffee, lunch, or a quick call. Low-pressure, their choice of time. The goal isn't business. It's to actually get to know the person behind the handshake.
5
The meeting · listen more than you talk
Conduct the 1-to-1
Run a FORM conversation. Ask about the four things people actually care about, and remember what they tell you. This is where a contact becomes a relationship.
F · Family O · Occupation R · Recreation M · Motivation
6
24 hours after · close the loop
Great Meeting With You
Thank them, recap the one thing that mattered, and name a next step. A recap the day after a meeting is rare enough to be remembered.
7
1–2 weeks · give before you ask
Create Value
An introduction, a referral, a resource, an invite. Every referral is a debt, pay it forward or it dies. Be the person who gives first, and the giving comes back.
8
Day 30+ · stay top of mind for good
Long-Term Nurture
A quarterly rhythm of check-ins, value, and genuine interest. Networking is not who you know, it's who knows you, and staying known is a small thing done consistently.
From the Manifesto

The system is downstream of the belief.

The 8 steps are how. This is why, in his own words, from Danny Smith's Stop Wasting Handshakes Manifesto.

I believe
Relationships are built, not harvested.
A meaningful handshake requires more than charm. It requires attention. It requires memory. It requires the humility to ask good questions and the patience to hear honest answers.
I choose
To be present. To notice. To remember. To respond.
I choose to add value before asking for anything in return. The strongest relationships are built through repeated acts of faithfulness.
I will
Build systems that help me remember people well.
I will follow-through when others fade away. I will be known as someone who means what they say, does what they promise, and values others deeply.
I measure
Not by how many people I meet, but by how many I truly saw.
Not by how many cards I collected, but by how many relationships I honored. I will not waste handshakes. I will honor them.
