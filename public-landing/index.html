Manifesto

Ten beliefs that change the way you network. Not theory. The operating commandments of every person who turned a business card into a million dollars of business.

I.
The handshake is the seed, not the sale.
Selling at first contact kills the relationship before it starts. The handshake's only job is to earn the next conversation.
II.
Follow through is the harvest.
The fortune isn't in who you meet. It's in who you call back, write to, refer, and remember on day forty-five.
III.
You can't control results. You can only control activity.
Outcomes are downstream of inputs. Make the inputs visible, automatic, weekly. The outcomes take care of themselves.
IV.
A weak network in motion beats a strong network at rest.
Consistent small action compounds. Sporadic big action evaporates. Pick the habit, not the heroics.
V.
Most people forget. Few follow up. Almost no one follows through.
The bar is on the floor. Eight intentional touches over forty-five days makes you unforgettable in any room.
VI.
Every referral is a debt. Pay it forward or it dies.
When someone sends you business, you owe two people — the one who sent, and the one who arrived. Treat both like the only one.
VII.
Networking is not who you know. It's who knows you.
Names in your phone don't pay you. You pay when you stay top of mind in theirs.
VIII.
If you can't measure it, you can't improve it.
The Scorecard exists because feelings lie about how active you actually are. Numbers don't.
IX.
The CRM does what your memory won't.
Eight steps, forty-five days, every contact. Systems beat willpower every week of the year.
X.
Do the small thing daily. Build something other people can't.
One year of disciplined networking is a relationship moat your competition will spend a decade trying to dig.
How it works

Three habits, compounded weekly.

Chase the points. Log the points. Stay accountable for the points. A loop simple enough to sustain, powerful enough most can't.

01
Chase at least 150 weekly points
150 is the floor that separates the disciplined from the dabblers. Every meeting, FORM conversation, follow-through, intro, and referral earns points. Get to 150 every week and the network builds itself.
02
Log the points
If it's not logged, it didn't happen. The Scorecard takes 30 seconds a day. Tap the activities you did, watch your tier climb in real time, and never wonder what kind of week you had again.
03
Be accountable to someone for the points
A score nobody sees is a score that slides. Share your weekly recap with a coach, a partner, or your team. Accountability is what turns a habit into a system.
